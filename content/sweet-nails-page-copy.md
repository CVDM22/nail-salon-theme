## About

**Page title:** About Sweet Nails

Sweet Nails started with a simple idea: a manicure should feel like a treat, not a chore. Tucked away at 24 Fake Street, 75000 Paris, our studio pairs the precision of a proper nail technician with the kind of colour, detail, and finish you'd want to show off.

Every set starts with the basics done right — shaping, cuticle care, a base that actually lasts — before our artists get to the part they love: gel finishes in colours you'll want to wear on repeat, hand-painted designs that hold up to real life, and sculpted extensions built to fit your hands, not a mould.

We sterilize every tool in a hospital-grade autoclave between clients, because a treat should never come with a trade-off.

Whether you're stopping in for a classic polish change or booking two hours with one of our nail artists for a custom set, you'll leave with hands you keep glancing at.

## FAQ

**Page title:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?**
We recommend booking ahead through our Book Now page to guarantee your preferred artist and time — walk-ins are welcome when a slot is open, but appointments get priority.

**How far in advance should I book?**
For a classic manicure or pedicure, a day or two ahead is usually plenty. For sculpted extensions, hand-painted nail art, or a specific artist, we'd suggest booking 3–5 days in advance, especially on weekends.

**What's your cancellation policy?**
We ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows may be subject to a 50% service fee — details are on the confirmation email you'll receive after booking.

**Can I request a specific nail artist?**
Yes — each of our artists has their own specialty (gel finishes, hand-painted art, sculpted extensions), and you can choose who you'd like to book with directly on the Book Now page.

**How long do appointments take?**
It depends on the service: a classic manicure runs about 30 minutes, while sculpted extensions or detailed nail art can take up to 90 minutes. Exact durations are listed next to each service on our Services & Pricing page.

**How do you sterilize your tools?**
Every tool is cleaned and sterilized in a hospital-grade autoclave between clients, and single-use items (files, buffers) are discarded after one use. We never reuse tools without sterilizing them first.

**Do you sell gift cards?**
Yes — gift cards are available in €25, €50, and €100 denominations in our online shop and make a good last-minute gift.

**What if I have a nail allergy or sensitivity?**
Let us know when you book, or mention it to your artist before your service starts — we can adjust products or do a patch test in advance if needed.

**Do you take card payments, or cash only?**
We accept credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Cash is also welcome in-studio.
